## Method notes

- Tone: WCAG contrast ratio between the two role colours. FAIL < 3:1 (the
  non-text distinguishability bar), WARN between 3:1 and 4.5:1. Ratio is a
  lightness measure — it deliberately says nothing about hue.
- CVD: `differenceScore` (0–100) under each chromatic dichromacy in `CVD_TYPES`
  threshold `30` — the same convention as the site's own
  "Are two colors distinguishable?" tool on `/color-blindness-simulator`.
- Construction: `companions.ts` promises split-complement hues with
  lightness chosen to contrast the primary's value; a retint or a washed-out
  companion breaks that promise regardless of ratios.
- Gamut: `331` outputs sit on the sRGB boundary — `oklchToHex`
  reduces chroma to fit while preserving hue and lightness, so these are
  saturation losses, not hue shifts.
